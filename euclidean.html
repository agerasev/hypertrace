Technical details



Drag to look; Tab locks the mouse. WASD moves; Space/C moves up/down; Q/E rolls. Scroll zooms; R resets. The viewer pauses when hidden or outside the page’s visible area. Open viewer separately.


What to observe
Look through the glass sphere at the cube and backdrop, then compare the backdrop’s reflection with the direct view.
  • As in every geometry, free rays follow intrinsic straight lines (geodesics). In Euclidean Cartesian coordinates these also appear as straight lines; the distortion through the yellow sphere comes from refraction.
  • The backdrop combines diffuse and specular reflection. Its tiles use physical cell dimensions, shared with the horosphere patterns in the hyperbolic example.
  • The sphere and cube have the same approximate scale as objects in the curved examples, making camera movement comparable.
